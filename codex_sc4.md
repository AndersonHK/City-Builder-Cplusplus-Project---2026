# Codex SC4 north star

Snapshot: 2026-04-08

## Why SC4 matters here
SC4 and SC2000 matter because they made cities feel like the visible result of invisible pressures. That emotional target stays central even as the implementation becomes more modern.

## What should be preserved
- a tile-based city model that stays legible at large scale
- indirect mayor-scale control through zoning, infrastructure, value, pollution, and other city statistics
- the sense that neighborhoods develop character because different pressures are interacting
- the pleasure of watching order and chaos emerge from systems

## What should be improved
- remove old technical handicaps instead of preserving them as accidental design rules
- make the simulation surfaces more inspectable and explainable
- allow much richer presentation, including potential 3D, without requiring full object simulation
- scale to much larger maps through cache-aware statistical tile simulation instead of walkers-as-truth design

## Final-state philosophy
The target is a statistic-driven city builder that feels like SC4's ambition continued forward into a cleaner technical foundation. The city should feel alive because the systems are coherent and the presentation expresses them well, not because every tiny thing is individually simulated in real time.

## One-sentence north star
Build a modern successor to SC4 and SC2000 that keeps the tile-based, systems-driven magic while finally giving it the clarity, scalability, and technical headroom it always deserved.
